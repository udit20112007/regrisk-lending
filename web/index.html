What is a lending partnership worth when the rules can change?

Fintechs earn fees by finding borrowers for banks, and often guarantee the first losses. This dashboard measures how often RBI actions hit fintech share prices and how hard, then prices that risk into the deal.

Method and limits

What the numbers rest on, and where to be careful.

Event study

For each stock, a line is fitted against the Nifty 50 on 120 calm trading days before the event. The abnormal return is the actual return minus what that line predicts, added up from the first day investors could react.

Lending deal

An EMI loan with defaults spread evenly over its life. The fintech's guarantee is a deposit capped by RBI at 5% of the amount disbursed, invoked 120 days after a missed payment and not refilled once used.

Fees, costs and default rates are illustrative.

Limits

  • Few events, and some overlap in time.
  • Reading share-price damage as lost loan volume is an assumption. The slider above tests it.
  • The model has no term for a lender wanting the fintech to keep money at risk.
